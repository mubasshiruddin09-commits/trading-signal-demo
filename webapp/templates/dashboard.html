Non-repainting signal guarantee: The Confluence Score, buy/sell direction, and price shown above are always locked to the last fully closed candle — never the live, still-forming one. This means once a signal is posted, it is mathematically final and cannot change, flip, or "repaint" later, regardless of how the market moves afterward. If the current candle hasn't closed yet, its live price is shown separately as context only and is never used to generate the official signal.

Methodology note: This dashboard shows the confluence scorer and backtest gate exactly as specified — no bare buy/sell verdict, and no signal reaches "passed" status without clearing an out-of-sample validation check on expectancy. Exit logic uses real ATR-based stop-loss and 1:10 risk/reward take-profit targets, walked forward bar-by-bar, net of modeled trading fees and slippage. A "not yet passing" gate means the system is correctly refusing to trust a setup with too little data or negative expectancy — not a bug. Auto-execution is intentionally not connected in this build. Historical candle caching, a background alert worker, and persistent per-user settings are planned next-phase infrastructure work.
